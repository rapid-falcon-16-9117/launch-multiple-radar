How a launch gets its price

The snapshot grades its own homework. For every category, the radar learns what the market already pays per dollar of TVL from the protocols whose tokens do trade, then prices a pre-token protocol the same way — adjusted only for what the snapshot says about that protocol itself.

  1. BaseTVL × category multiple — the median market cap ÷ TVL of trading peers, samples capped at 8× so one mania cannot price a category

  3. Maturity× up to 1.5 for years operated with no token — scarcity compounds
  4. Reach× +4% per extra chain, capped at 1.25 — wider day-one distribution
